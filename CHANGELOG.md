# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-07)
- The shared Critter look (with Critter VTT and Critter Notes): surfaces lift by light instead of lines, drawn selects and checkboxes,
  Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, a see-through title bar showing the mark, the full logo in the header, glows only on hover.
- New logo and app icons; the logo and the title-bar mark follow your highlight colour.
- Appearance: light or dark, surface tint, picture colour and font pairings. The cover spills its colours into the play bar, graded so text stays readable.
- One flat button style across the Critter apps: 8px corners, no outlines, gradients or lift; the main action and pressed toggles are filled with your highlight.
- Narrow windows fold their title-bar buttons into ⋯ (✕ always stays).
- "Old radio" no longer makes the music louder: loudness matching listens through a light hearing weighting, so every effect sits within about half a decibel of the dry music.
- Critter is now called Critter VTT in the text.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-06)
- First release: live music for a Critter VTT table over WebRTC, playlists, sound pads, soundscapes, effects, Discord and Fluxer voice bots, German interface, guided tours.
